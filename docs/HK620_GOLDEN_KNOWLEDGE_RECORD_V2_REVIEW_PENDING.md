## 5. Process Logic

Source: Founder Interview / 2026-07-06  
Review Status: review_pending  
Public Use: No

The interview explains HK620's process logic as:

```text
edge banding first
↓
grooving second
↓
cutting last
```

Review-pending facts:

1. The process should start with edge banding.
2. Grooving follows edge banding.
3. Cutting is performed after edge banding and grooving.
4. Cutting first may make later edge banding difficult because short strips are harder to feed, hold, and press stably.
5. Ordinary edge banding machines may have short-strip processing limitations.

Notes:

The exact public statement about short-strip size limits requires engineering confirmation.
