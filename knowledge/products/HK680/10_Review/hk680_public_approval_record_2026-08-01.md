## Approval Scope

### Approved for Public Use

The following information has been reviewed and approved by the CEO for use in public-facing content (website articles, marketing materials, product pages):

1. **Technical Specifications** (all parameters from official SYUTECH page):
   - Product Model: HK680
   - Product Type: Servo edge banding machine for panel furniture
   - Glue Process: PUR and EVA
   - Panel length (min): 150 mm (corner trimming: 45x300 mm)
   - Panel width (min): 50 mm
   - Edge band width: 10-60 mm
   - Edge band thickness: 0.4-3 mm
   - Feeding speed: 16 / 18 / 22 m/min (3-speed)
   - Installed power: 17.56 kW
   - Air pressure: 0.7-0.9 MPa
   - Machine size: 6700 x 710 x 1628 mm
   - Machine weight: 2500 kg
   - Voltage: 380V/50Hz (CEO confirmed)
   - Warranty: 1 year (CEO confirmed)
   - Delivery time: 30 days (CEO confirmed)
   - Applicable materials: MDF, particleboard, plywood (CEO confirmed)

2. **Product Features** (6 items):
   - Servo-controlled fine trimming, corner rounding, and scraping
   - Complete process: spray, milling, gluing, end trimming, fine trimming, corner rounding, scraping, flat scrap, cleaning, buffing
   - Computer touch screen with internet remote control, pre-heating, and memory function
   - Inovance (Huichuan) PLC + Inverter + Control System
   - Independent air storage tank for stable pneumatic supply
   - Diamond pre-milling cutter with built-in dust blower

3. **Component Brand List** (14 items from official page)

4. **FAQ** (6 Q&A entries)

5. **Product Positioning Statements**:
   - HK680 is a servo panel edge banding machine for panel furniture
   - HK680 targets panel furniture manufacturers (MDF, particleboard, plywood)
   - HK680 uses both PUR and EVA glue types
   - HK680 is NOT a profile/skeleton edge banding machine (that is HK620)

6. **Application Scenarios**:
   - Panel furniture edge banding
   - Servo precision edge banding for high-quality furniture panels
   - OEM/ODM customizable edge banding production
