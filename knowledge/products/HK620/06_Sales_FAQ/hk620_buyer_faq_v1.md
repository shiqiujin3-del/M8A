## FAQ Entries

### Q1: Is HK620 a normal panel edge banding machine?

**A:** No. HK620 is positioned for skeleton lines, decorative strips, door-line components, and profile-style edge banding. It is not a generic replacement for every panel edge banding machine.

### Q2: What glue process does HK620 use?

**A:** HK620 uses PUR glue edge banding, which provides strong bonding suitable for visible profile components.

### Q3: Can HK620 handle all line-shaped workpieces?

**A:** Not automatically. HK620 is suitable for various line and decorative-strip workpieces, including straight-edge and angled profile shapes. Unusual profiles should still be confirmed with samples.

### Q4: Is sample confirmation required?

**A:** For serious buying decisions, yes. Buyers should send photos, dimensions, material information, and production expectations before final quotation.

### Q5: Is HK620 already in production?

**A:** Yes. HK620 has completed development and testing cycles and has entered batch production.

### Q6: What are the packaging dimensions?

**A:** HK620 is packaged as 8.3m (length) x 0.9m (width) x 1.6m (height). Buyers should confirm container fit and unloading requirements based on these dimensions.
