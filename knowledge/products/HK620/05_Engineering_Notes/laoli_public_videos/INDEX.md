# Laoli Public Video Engineering Knowledge

Source: 东莞顾态老李 publicly published videos  
Product Scope: HK620 and related woodworking / edge banding engineering knowledge  
Status: source intake ready

## Purpose

This directory stores Engineering Knowledge Cards derived from publicly available videos.

Important boundary:

1. Do not copy videos into M8A.
2. Do not download videos.
3. Do not treat extracted knowledge as approved product knowledge.
4. Every extracted card must enter Engineering Review Queue.
5. Golden Knowledge can be updated only after human review.

## Source Link Intake

Add source links to:

```text
VIDEO_SOURCE_LIST.md
```

Each source should include:

- video title
- public URL
- platform
- publish date if known
- uploader/account name
- product mentioned if known

## Extraction Fields

Each video should be converted into one Engineering Knowledge Card with:

1. Theme
2. Products involved
3. Process flow
4. Engineering experience
5. Common mistakes
6. FAQ
7. Adjustment tips
8. Applicable scenarios
9. Confidence level
10. Whether 老李 confirmation is required

## Review Rule

All cards start as:

```text
needs_review
```

No card may update HK620 Golden Knowledge until reviewed.
