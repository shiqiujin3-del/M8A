## Service FAQ Entries

### Q1: What information is needed before quotation?

**A:** Buyers should provide:
1. Product photos or short videos
2. Cross-section drawings or dimensions
3. Workpiece length, width, and thickness
4. Base material
5. Edge banding material
6. Glue or bonding requirements
7. Daily production target
8. Voltage and workshop layout
9. Destination country or delivery port

### Q2: Why is sample review important?

**A:** Two factories may both say they process "door lines," but their actual workpieces can be very different. Profile shape, material, surface treatment, edge tape, glue requirement, and final quality standard all affect the machine recommendation. HK620 should be sold through a confirmation process, not only through a fixed catalog description.

### Q3: What is the delivery cycle?

**A:** Approximately 30 days. (Approved for public use from Post 481 technical specifications)

### Q4: What is the machine's development and testing history?

**A:** HK620 has gone through approximately three months of development and approximately three months of testing, and has already entered batch production. (Approved for public use from Post 481)
