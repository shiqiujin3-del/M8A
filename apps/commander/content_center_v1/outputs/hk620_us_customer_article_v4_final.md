## Overview

For manufacturers producing skeleton door profiles, decorative mouldings, door casing strips, furniture trim, and other long narrow wooden profiles, edge banding is not only a surface finishing step. It directly affects finished appearance, batch consistency, delivery stability, and customer acceptance.

The HK620 is a dedicated skeleton-line and decorative-strip edge banding machine designed for profile-type workpieces. It is different from a standard panel edge banding machine. Its value is in helping door factories, furniture factories, and decorative profile producers improve edge banding stability on visible profile components.

## Why Profile Edge Banding Needs a Dedicated Machine

Skeleton door profiles and decorative strips are often long, narrow, and visible after installation. Unlike flat panels, profile parts may have different cross sections, decorative surfaces, and strict appearance requirements.

When the wrong machine is used, factories may face:

- Unstable feeding on narrow or profiled parts
- Inconsistent glue lines
- Uneven trimming quality
- More manual repair required
- Visible differences within the same batch
- Higher rejection risk during customer inspection

For production managers, these problems become delivery, labor, and after-sales problems — not just quality problems.

## What HK620 Is Used For

HK620 is suitable for factories working with:

- Skeleton door profiles
- Decorative door strips
- Door casing and door frame profiles

If a factory only processes standard rectangular panels, a conventional panel edge banding machine may still be the first option. If the factory handles skeleton lines, decorative strips, or furniture profile components, HK620 becomes a more relevant machine to evaluate.

## FAQ

### Is HK620 a normal panel edge banding machine?

No. HK620 is positioned for skeleton lines, decorative strips, door-line components, and profile-style edge banding. It is not a generic replacement for every panel edge banding machine.

### What glue process does HK620 use?

HK620 uses PUR glue edge banding, which provides strong bonding suitable for visible profile components.

### Can HK620 handle all line-shaped workpieces?

Not automatically. HK620 is suitable for various line and decorative-strip workpieces, including straight-edge and angled profile shapes. Unusual profiles should still be confirmed with samples.

### Is sample confirmation required?

For serious buying decisions, yes. Buyers should send photos, dimensions, material information, and production expectations before final quotation.

### Is HK620 already in production?

Yes. HK620 has completed development and testing cycles and has entered batch production.

### What are the packaging dimensions?

HK620 is packaged as 8.3m (length) × 0.9m (width) × 1.6m (height). Buyers should confirm container fit and unloading requirements based on these dimensions.
